FairProcess is a deterministic due-process audit engine built on 24 validated California property statutes. Every anomaly flagged has a direct citation and evidence graph back to the source document — attachable to a court motion without legal risk.

FairProcess extracts parcel GIS data, builds evidence provenance graphs, and audits government notices for statutory violations in under 15 minutes. Export complete, court-ready timeline reports directly into your existing client files.
